SMART OFFERS (BOGO MODULE) – SRS DOCUMENT

1. OVERVIEW
This system is used to create Buy X Get Y (BOGO) offers in WooCommerce.
The admin can easily create offers such as:
- Buy 2 → Get 1 Free
- Buy Shoes → Get Socks Free
- Buy 3 → Get 20% Discount


2. BASIC SETTINGS

Status:
- Used to enable or disable the offer
- Active → Offer will be applied
- Inactive → Offer will not be applied

Title:
- Name of the offer (for admin use only)
- Not displayed on the frontend


3. BOGO LOGIC

Buy Quantity (X):
- Minimum quantity the customer must purchase
- Example: 2 → Customer must buy at least 2 items

Get Quantity (Y):
- Quantity the customer will receive as free or discounted
- Example: Buy 2 → Get 1

Apply Mode:

Repeat Mode:
- Offer will be applied multiple times based on quantity
- Example:
  Buy 2 → Get 1
  Buy 4 → Get 2
  Buy 6 → Get 3

Once Mode:
- Offer will be applied only once
- Example:
  Buy 2 → Get 1
  Buy 10 → Still get only 1


4. REWARD SETTINGS

Reward Type:

Free Product:
- Customer receives a product for free

Percentage Discount:
- Customer receives a percentage (%) discount

Fixed Discount:
- Customer receives a fixed amount discount (₹)

Select Free Product:
- The product that will be given for free

Discount Value:
- The value of the discount
- Example:
  20 → 20%
  100 → ₹100

Apply Discount On:

Cheapest Product:
- The cheapest product in the cart

Highest Price Product:
- The most expensive product in the cart

Specific Product:
- Admin manually selects which product gets the discount

Select Discount Product:
- (Only for specific option)
- The product on which the discount will be applied


5. ADVANCED SETTINGS

Auto Add Free Product:
- ON → System automatically adds the free product to the cart
- OFF → User must manually add the product

Limit Per Order:
- Maximum number of times the offer can be applied in one order
- Example:
  Limit = 2 → Offer applies only 2 times


6. TRIGGER SETTINGS

Trigger Products:
- Products that must be purchased to activate the offer
- Example:
  Buying Shoes will trigger the offer

Exclude Products:
- Offer will not apply if these products are present in the cart

Minimum Quantity:
- Minimum quantity required in the cart to activate the offer


7. DISPLAY SETTINGS

Offer Message:
- Message shown before the offer is unlocked
- Example:
  "Add 1 more item to get FREE gift"

Success Message:
- Message shown after the offer is applied
- Example:
  "Free gift added successfully"

Show Progress Bar:
- Displays progress to the user
- Example:
  70% complete

Device Control:
- Control visibility on mobile or desktop devices


8. SYSTEM FLOW

User adds product to cart
↓
System checks trigger conditions
↓
If conditions are met
↓
Reward is applied (Free / Discount)
↓
Cart is updated


9. EXAMPLE

Setup:
- Buy Quantity = 2
- Get Quantity = 1
- Reward = Free Product (Socks)
- Trigger Product = Shoes

Result:
- Customer buys 2 Shoes
- Customer receives 1 Socks for free


10. IMPORTANT NOTES

- Reward product must be selected
- Discount value must be set correctly
- Apply mode must be selected properly
- Backend logic must be implemented for the offer to work


SMART OFFERS (BOGO MODULE) – SRS DOCUMENT

1. OVERVIEW
यह सिस्टम WooCommerce में Buy X Get Y (BOGO) offers बनाने के लिए उपयोग किया जाएगा।
Admin आसानी से offers बना सकता है जैसे:
- 2 खरीदो → 1 फ्री पाओ
- Shoes खरीदो → Socks फ्री
- 3 खरीदो → 20% discount पाओ


2. BASIC SETTINGS

Status:
- Offer को ON / OFF करता है
- Active → Offer लागू होगा
- Inactive → Offer लागू नहीं होगा

Title:
- Offer का नाम (सिर्फ admin के लिए)
- Frontend पर दिखाई नहीं देता


3. BOGO LOGIC

Buy Quantity (X):
- ग्राहक को कितनी quantity खरीदनी होगी
- Example: 2 → ग्राहक को 2 item खरीदने होंगे

Get Quantity (Y):
- ग्राहक को कितनी quantity free या discount में मिलेगी
- Example: Buy 2 → Get 1

Apply Mode:

Repeat Mode:
- Offer बार-बार लागू होगा
- Example:
  Buy 2 → Get 1
  Buy 4 → Get 2
  Buy 6 → Get 3

Once Mode:
- Offer सिर्फ एक बार लागू होगा
- Example:
  Buy 2 → Get 1
  Buy 10 → फिर भी सिर्फ 1 मिलेगा


4. REWARD SETTINGS

Reward Type:

Free Product:
- ग्राहक को एक product free मिलेगा

Percentage Discount:
- ग्राहक को प्रतिशत (%) में discount मिलेगा

Fixed Discount:
- ग्राहक को निश्चित राशि (₹) का discount मिलेगा

Select Free Product:
- यह वह product है जो customer को free मिलेगा

Discount Value:
- कितना discount देना है
- Example:
  20 → 20%
  100 → ₹100

Apply Discount On:

Cheapest Product:
- Cart का सबसे सस्ता product

Highest Price Product:
- Cart का सबसे महंगा product

Specific Product:
- Admin खुद select करेगा कि discount किस product पर लगे

Select Discount Product:
- (Specific option चुनने पर)
- जिस product पर discount लगेगा


5. ADVANCED SETTINGS

Auto Add Free Product:
- ON → System खुद free product cart में add करेगा
- OFF → User manually add करेगा

Limit Per Order:
- एक order में maximum कितनी बार offer लागू होगा
- Example:
  Limit = 2 → केवल 2 बार offer मिलेगा


6. TRIGGER SETTINGS

Trigger Products:
- कौन सा product खरीदने पर offer लागू होगा
- Example:
  Shoes खरीदने पर offer trigger होगा

Exclude Products:
- ये product cart में होने पर offer लागू नहीं होगा

Minimum Quantity:
- Offer activate होने के लिए minimum quantity required


7. DISPLAY SETTINGS

Offer Message:
- Offer activate होने से पहले दिखाया जाने वाला message
- Example:
  "Add 1 more item to get FREE gift"

Success Message:
- Offer apply होने के बाद दिखाया जाने वाला message
- Example:
  "Free gift added successfully"

Show Progress Bar:
- User को progress दिखाने के लिए
- Example:
  70% complete

Device Control:
- Offer को mobile या desktop पर दिखाना या छिपाना


8. SYSTEM FLOW

User product add करता है
↓
System trigger condition check करता है
↓
Condition match होने पर reward apply होता है
↓
Cart update हो जाता है


9. EXAMPLE

Setup:
- Buy Quantity = 2
- Get Quantity = 1
- Reward = Free Product (Socks)
- Trigger Product = Shoes

Result:
- Customer 2 Shoes खरीदेगा
- उसे 1 Socks free मिलेगा


10. IMPORTANT NOTES

- Reward product select करना जरूरी है
- Discount value सही देना जरूरी है
- Apply mode सही चुनना जरूरी है
- Backend logic लागू होना जरूरी है तभी offer काम करेगा
